There is always something happening in Brunei. Find out about local celebrations, important events and where to get more information.

Discover Local Events

Brunei celebrates different events throughout the year. These occasions are a great way to experience the country's traditions and culture.

National Day

23 February

Brunei celebrates its independence with national ceremonies, performances and community activities.

Hari Raya Aidilfitri

Date changes each year

Families and friends come together after Ramadan to celebrate with food, gatherings and open houses.

His Majesty's Birthday

15 July

This special occasion celebrates the birthday of His Majesty the Sultan of Brunei, with official ceremonies and public celebrations.

Chinese New Year

Date changes each year

The Chinese Bruneian community celebrates with family gatherings, traditional food and cultural customs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Brunei safe for tourists?

Brunei is generally considered a peaceful destination. Visitors should still take normal travel precautions and respect local laws.

What should I wear when visiting mosques?

Visitors should dress modestly when entering mosques. Some places also provide robes for visitors.

What currency does Brunei use?

Brunei uses the Brunei dollar (BND). Singapore dollars are also accepted at the same value.

What language do people speak?

Malay is the official language. English is also commonly used, especially in business and education.
