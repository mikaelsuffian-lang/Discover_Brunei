Plan Your Visit

Simple tips to help you enjoy your time in Brunei.

Good to Know Before You Go

Currency

Brunei uses the Brunei dollar (BND). The Singapore dollar is accepted at par under the Currency Interchangeability Agreement.

Getting Around

Travel options include taxis, Dart ride-hailing, public buses and water taxis. Check current routes and availability.

Weather

Brunei is tropical, warm and humid throughout the year. Bring light clothing and prepare for rain.

Local Etiquette

Dress respectfully, especially at religious sites, and remove shoes when requested.

Visa & Entry

Entry requirements depend on your nationality. Check passport validity and visa rules before travelling.

Immigration Department →

Accommodation

Visitors can choose from hotels in the capital to eco-lodges and accommodation near nature attractions.

Best Time to Visit

Brunei can be visited throughout the year. February to April is often relatively drier, while heavier rainfall is more common around the later part of the year. Weather varies, so check the forecast close to your travel dates.

For outdoor activities, plan early starts to avoid the strongest midday heat and bring rain protection.

Getting to Brunei

By Air

Most international visitors arrive at Brunei International Airport (BWN), near Bandar Seri Begawan. Check available routes and flight schedules with airlines before booking.

Royal Brunei Airlines →

By Land

Brunei has road border crossings with the Malaysian state of Sarawak. Travellers should check the operating status, opening hours and entry requirements of the chosen border post.

Entry requirements →

From the Airport

From Brunei International Airport, visitors can continue to Bandar Seri Begawan by taxi or ride-hailing service. Allow extra time for traffic and check fares before travelling.
